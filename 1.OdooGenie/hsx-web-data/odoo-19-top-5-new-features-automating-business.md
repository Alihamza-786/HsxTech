# Odoo 19: Top 5 New Features Automating Business Operations With Odoo AI and More!

Odoo 19 is now here, and it’s stronger than ever. With user-friendly improvements and revolutionary AI-based tools, this version is crafted to make business processes easier and more efficient. As a business owner, tech-savvy individual, or busy Odoo user, these new features are guaranteed to improve your operations and elevate Odoo’s functionality to the next level.
Here, we’ll take a look at the top five things Odoo 19 has to offer and how they can enable your business to achieve more. And, we’ll demonstrate real-world examples to show you their effect.

## Smarter Purchase Orders with Recommendations

Purchase orders have never been easier to manage! One of the most thrilling features in Odoo 19 is the power to streamline purchase orders due to smart recommendations.

### What Does It Do?
Odoo 19 offers suggestions straight into the purchase order catalog, pinning down competitors with low price points and in stock levels. It cross-compares sales history and inventory on hand, which makes for sounder purchasing choices.

In contrast to classical min-max restocking rules, it makes it possible to respond to seasonality as well as the rise and fall of business activity without having to deal with any hassle.

### Use Case
Picture operating an online store. You’re running low on inventory for a best-seller, and Odoo’s AI suggests the perfect vendor who not only carries the inventory you require but also at a better price. No spreadsheet calculations needed!
This feature is a game-changer and appears to save time and money for companies in all industries.

## Odoo AI for Smart Task Assignment

Organizing tasks is critical to operational effectiveness. Odoo 19 gives your task management a boost by utilizing AI-driven automation to assign tasks according to certain conditions.

### What Does It Do?
With Odoo AI, you can automate task assignments and delegation. Integrating task assignment into automated processes eliminates manual management and ensures proper tasks reach the proper team members.

### Use Case
Imagine this: A customer support representative gets an email asking why an order is delayed. From the email content, Odoo AI will automatically assign the job to a logistics team member. This eliminates the delay and manual work that goes into task delegation, which means quicker resolution times for your customers.

## Enrich Customer Records with Odoo AI

Odoo 19 now enables users to add properties to any record, including customers, using an AI-powered field that auto-completes based on your input. This feature simplifies data enrichment and ensures a customer’s profile always reflects the most accurate and relevant details.

With this functionality, AI processes your inputs and provides thoughtful suggestions to enhance records. For instance, it can suggest added fields like customer likes, industry, or buying history based on current data points.

### Use Case
Prior to Odoo 19, a customer profile could contain simple contact information and their most recent purchase. Today, AI can propose extra fields like “Favorite Products” or “Communication Preferences,” giving a full picture of the customer in seconds.
This more intelligent data enrichment enables companies to provide personalized experiences, foster loyalty, and make data-driven decisions with greater ease.

## Add New Fields with Enrichment Using Only a Prompt

Odoo AI lifts customisation to new heights by allowing you to add entirely new fields on records with just a simple question. Companies are now able to customise Odoo to their precise requirements without complicated coding or development efforts.

Just enter what you require in a prompt, and Odoo generates enriched fields for your records. For instance, you can include data like KPIs, certifications, or product-specific metrics so that everyone remains on the same page and informed.

### Use Case
A new hire updates their skills in the employee app, sharing information about certain certifications or skills they’ve just learned. With a simple prompt, the AI inserts an “Expertise” field right into their profile and auto-fills it with useful context. It’s that easy!

## AI-Powered Contextual Messaging

Communication has just become easier with Odoo AI, which writes messages based on the complete context of a conversation and lead history. The feature enables sales and customer service teams to create customized responses in a quicker time than ever. 

When interacting with leads or customers, Odoo AI considers past interactions, inclinations, and history to write personalized messages. No more scrambling for notes or getting it wrong with impersonal replies. Odoo ensures you get the tone right every time.

### Use Case
A sales rep ready to follow up on a lead can use Odoo AI to craft a message mentioning a previous conversation and offering something related to the lead’s expressed interests. The outcome? A professional, customized email in a matter of moments, rather than the customary time, raising the prospect of closing the sale.
