# How to Create and Manage Invoices in Odoo Accounting

A businessperson knows that managing invoices will keep the company running smoothly. But we get it – invoicing can get a bit overwhelming. Odoo Accounting simplifies invoicing so you can manage invoices easily, minimize mistakes, and save time.

Throughout this guidebook, I am going to take you step-by-step, through how to set up and manage invoices in Odoo Accounting, so payments never fall through your hands. If you are new to Odoo or need a reminder on how to invoice, you’ll be ready in minutes!

## Creating an Invoice in Odoo Accounting

If you have already installed Odoo Accounting, let us proceed to the fun part: creating and managing invoices! Here is how you can do it:

### Step 1: Create a Customer Invoice
1. Go to the “Invoicing” Tab: From your dashboard, click on “Invoicing” and then click on “Create.”
2. Enter Customer Details: Select the customer from your database or create a new customer record. You can also assign the invoice to a sales order directly if you want.
3. Select Products and Services: Select the products or services you are billing. Odoo maintains your product stock in place, so it is very easy to select and add the products. You can even add a description, tax, and discount here.
4. Define Payment Terms: Choose the payment terms (e.g., Net 30, cash payment) so the customer knows when to pay.

### Step 2: Create a Customer Invoice
With Odoo, it is easy to customize your invoices with just one click. Here’s how to do it:

1. Choose a Template: Choose from numerous pre-designed templates based on your brand. You can input your logo, contact information, and other brand information into the templates.
2. Add Terms and Conditions: Add any other conditions, such as late fees, at the end of the invoice.
3. Insert Taxes and Discounts: Odoo has simplified calculations for taxes. Any discount provided above the bill, you can insert the same directly.

### Step 3: Save and Finalize the Invoice
Once you have your invoice ready, you save the same as a draft for verification, or you simply confirm the same. Whenever you confirm, your invoice will get activated and you can send the same to your customer directly from Odoo.

## Managing Your Invoices in Odoo Accounting

Now that you know how to create an invoice, let us discuss how to process the invoice when it reaches you.

### Invoice Statuses and Tracking
In Odoo, invoices are assigned statuses whereby you can, at a glance, tell where each one stands. The most critical ones are:

- Draft: The invoice is prepared but not yet sent to the customer.
- Sent: Invoice has been sent to customer but not paid.
- Open: Invoice has been sent to the customer and waiting to pay.
- Paid: Invoice is paid.

You can browse and search your invoices by status, so you will always be aware of what is happening with your payments.

### Editing and Altering Invoices
You might need to alter an invoice once it is posted. Here’s how:

- Create Draft Invoices: You can edit invoices with a draft status at any time.
- Validated Invoices: Once confirmed, an invoice is locked, but you can still pay or change the due date. Significant changes need you to begin a new invoice.

### Managing Recurring Invoices
If you have subscription-based business or regular customers, Odoo allows you to automate regular invoices. Simply make a template of a regular invoice and set the frequency (yearly, monthly, weekly). Odoo will automatically create the invoices and even send them to your customers on your behalf, saving your time and effort.

## Processing Payments and Matching Against Invoices

Recording payments and reconciling them with invoices is an important aspect of processing invoices in Odoo Accounting. This is how it’s done:

### Recording Payments
You can simply record payments in Odoo as soon as your customer has made the payment. Here’s how:

1. Access the Payment Menu: In the Invoicing section, click on “Payments” and then “Register Payment.”
2. Select the Invoice: Choose what the invoice payment is for.
3. Record the Payment: Choose the means of payment (bank, cash, etc.) and input the sum. Partial payments can be made when necessary.

### Payment Matching and Reconciliation
Odoo will automatically match payments with corresponding invoices. Reconcile manually in case of a mismatch. Your accounts remain constantly updated and correct in this manner.

## Automating Your Invoice Workflow in Odoo

Automation is one of the strongest features of Odoo Accounting. Here is how you can leverage it.

### Email Reminders and Alerts
Automate reminder emails for unpaid invoices. You can even automate follow-up so to receive payments at the top without even necessarily having to respond to it. Odoo lets you personalize email templates and send reminders as many times as you desire.

### Automating Recurring Invoices
As I mentioned, you can also have invoice templates with recurring ones. So, Odoo will invoice for you, whether it is a subscription that occurs a month or a long project that occurs for months. Once, Odoo will take care of the rest!
