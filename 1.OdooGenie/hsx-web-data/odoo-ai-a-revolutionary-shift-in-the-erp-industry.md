## 4 Core AI Features That Make Odoo AI a Game Changer

So, what’s new with Odoo 19? Four cornerstone features disrupt the way we think about ERP.

Odoo AI pushes wiser planning through predictive models to your business dashboards. Your historical data leads it to push you to plan-wise.

Real-world examples:

- Sales Forecasting: Estimate future sales considering season trend, lead-to-opportunity conversion percentage, and past.

This is probably the simplest manner in which Odoo AI turns ERP on its head — it gives users the ability to make proactive rather than reactive decisions.

Thanks to being able to generate by Odoo AI, goodbye writer’s block and snooze-inducing content creation.

Odoo AI can generate:

This not only reduces the time but also ensures consistency and quality of messages.

One of the most valuable aspects of Odoo AI is that it can automate repetitive work and suggest the next best action.

This smart automation is revolutionizing day-to-day business processes — and it’s at the heart of the newest trend in the ERP marketplace.
