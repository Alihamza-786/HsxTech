## Top Features of Odoo ERP for Car Companies

### Inventory Management
Odoo ERP has advanced systems for tracking inventory, like double-entry stock management and barcode scanning. Car companies can easily keep track of spare parts, tools, and supplies and ensure all needed products are in stock.

### Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
The dealership of cars and service center finds customer relationships very important. Odoo CRM is that part of the platform where businesses can maintain a history of customer dealings, follow-up plans, and plan some special marketing campaigns to make a customer loyal.

### Repair Management
Odoo ERP includes a repair module designed for businesses that offer vehicle maintenance services. It allows you to:
Create repair orders.
Track work in progress.
Manage spare part usage.
This ensures repair jobs are completed on time and with high accuracy.

### Sales and Quotation Management
For automotive dealerships, Odoo ERP simplifies sales workflows by generating engaging vehicle or part quotations. It also provides real-time updates, allowing your sales team to focus on closing deals rather than administrative tasks.

### Supply Chain and Vendors Management
Odoo's Purchase module streamlines supply chain management. Automate purchase orders, manage price lists for vendors, and monitor incoming shipments to make your supply chain efficient and transparent.

### Keeping accounts and money accounts
The ERP accounting module helps streamline billing, track payments, and produce financial statements. This not only saves time but also brings compliance with tax and regulation laws.

### Fleet Management
For companies with their own fleets, Odoo ERP helps track the fleet's movement, plan maintenance activities, and keep an eye on mileage all in one dashboard.
