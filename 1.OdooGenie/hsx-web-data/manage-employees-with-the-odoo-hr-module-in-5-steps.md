# How to Manage Employees with the Odoo HR Module in 5 Easy Steps

Managing people can be difficult, but it does not have to be. If you have employees broadcasting live and taking business on Odoo and are prepared to roll out your employees, you’ve come to the correct tutorial. This tutorial walks you through staffing management with the Odoo HR module in five easy, achievable steps.

Whether you are an HR director of a developing firm or an operations director with diversified responsibilities, if you implement these suggestions in practice, you can make your Odoo setup productive and maintain your employee management process smooth and hassle-free.

## Step 1: Centralize All Your Employee Data in One Place
### Why Centralization Matters
Face it: managing spreadsheets, email, and paper files is tiring—let alone error-ridden. You’ll be able to handle employees more effectively using the Odoo HR module since all the employee information is in one place.

### How to Set It Up
- Open the ‘Employee’ application from Odoo.
- Click Create to create a new employee.
- Enter essential details: full name, job title, work email address, department, and contact information.
- Also upload supporting documents like resumes, contracts, and certificates.

Time-Saver Tip: When you’re onboarding many employees at once, use the Import feature wisely. Import an Excel or CSV file, and Odoo will guide you through mapping the fields correctly. It’s a major time-saver when onboarding in bulk.

## Step 2: Adjust Employee Profiles to Your Business Needs

Each business is different—and yours should be different in your employee records. Odoo’s HR module allows you to handle employees the way that makes sense for your particular business, software company or manufacturing facility.

### What You Can Customize
- Include custom fields such as certifications, licenses, or emergency contacts.
- Track assigned work equipment for each worker
- Ticket employees by skill, location, or project
- Include internal notes only visible to the managers

### Why It Matters
Personal profiles assist you in hiring better, automating internal processes, and providing your whole team with a smooth experience.

Bonus: Design advanced custom fields and layouts with ease using Odoo Studio without writing a single line of code.

## Step 3: Automate Onboarding, Offboarding, and Everything In Between

One of the most amazing things about the Odoo HR module is how it manages the employee life cycle. From day one to the goodbye party, you can manage employees more reliably by automating key tasks.

### For Onboarding
- Automatically assign tasks to HR, IT, and managers
- Automatically attach key onboarding documents (e.g., welcome letter, employee handbook)
- Schedule reminders for follow-up and probation review

### For Offboarding
- Automatically trigger equipment return checklists
- Send exit interview surveys via email
- Automatic disabling of linked system accounts
- Automation removes the guesswork and smooths out your internal processes each time.

## Step 4: Track Time Off and Attendance with Minimal Effort

Holiday day and sick leave tracking can be a pain. Not anymore with Odoo. Employee attendance and leave balances can be tracked in the HR module.

### Time Off Management
- Establish different leave types (Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Parental Leave, etc.)
- Request time off through the employees’ self-service portal
- Approve or reject leaves with one click
- Real-time visibility into balance and history

### Attendance Tracking
Want more control over worked time? Add the Attendance module so employees can clock-in and clock-out from a computer, tablet, or biometric device.

Tip: Use reporting tools to track absenteeism patterns and ensure compliance with labor laws.

## Step 5: Track Performance and Create Smart HR Reports

If you wish to keep your star players and catch problems early on before they become humongous issues, then you need data. The Odoo HR module has everything you need to track performance and get employees on your radar in advance.

### Set Up Performance Reviews
- Automatic regular appraisals set and remind yourself when it is due
- Individual evaluation forms for various job roles
- Feedback from various sources—employees, peers, managers

### Analyze HR Data
- Create dashboards showing team headcount, turnover, absenteeism, and more
- Export the reports to Excel or PDF
- Make strategic decisions on recruitment, retention, and training based on these facts.

Facts regarding data-driven decision-making do not have to be a domain for big businesses only. With Odoo, any business can accomplish this.
