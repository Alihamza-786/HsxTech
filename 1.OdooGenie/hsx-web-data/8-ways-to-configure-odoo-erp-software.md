# 8 Ways to Configure Odoo ERP Software According to Your Specific Needs

If you’re considering switching to Odoo ERP software, you’re already on the path to simplifying your business processes and boosting productivity. One of the exciting aspects of Odoo ERP software is also one of its challenges: it’s very customizable. This flexibility in Odoo can make a big difference in how well the software works for you. However, knowing where to start or how to leverage that flexibility is not always easy.

Being the first person to have hands-on experience implementing the Odoo ERP software for businesses, I understand that no one else like me has been through this and figured out how this customization would make an excellent or sound system. If it is the latter, meaning an upgrade from some other ERP system or a first-time Odoo setup, let this post take you through what will suit the Odoo ERP setup you need.


## 1. Why Odoo ERP Software's Flexibility Matters for Your Business

The first thing I always remind business owners when they consider switching to Odoo ERP software is this: it’s not one size fits all. What makes Odoo very powerful is the flexibility that exists toward different industries and business models.

For instance, I recall my first experience when I joined a small retailing company eager to move out of QuickBooks and into Odoo—the whole team was afraid. Their current system was pretty primitive but would not support many of the more essential features with which they wanted to grow and expand.

One example of a characteristic that Odoo has in its software for which he was helped is flexibility in the inventory, account, and client relationship, a significant problem with which they had concerns. On their side, this was the biggest secret to seeing Odoo working: using an adapted rather than strictly off-the-shelf configuration.

The Odoo ERP software modules in the backend helped them implement an automated system that gave them better insights about their work and was easily scalable when they had to expand.

## 2. Know Your Needs Before You Begin to Customize

So, before diving into all the modules and features that appear in the Odoo ERP software, step back and consider your business needs. What do you want to achieve with this ERP system? Are you more interested in enhancing financial reporting or solving a problem mainly related to automatic sales orders and inventory? To describe my client, my client was a manufacturing firm.

All that was causing them an issue was the production flow, from raw products to finished ones, that they had at hand today. They did not need to use most modules available in Odoo, but something specific that could track material’s scheduled work orders or handle procurements. Now, we could have configured the Odoo to have only that which will fall within the scope of the needed business processes. Therefore, there shall be no room for confusion that will automatically give way to some level of simplification.

### Assessing Your Existing Systems
Write down the current systems if you come from another ERP or spreadsheet. Overlap a few of them with the features provided by the Odoo ERP software. Write down functionalities you can’t function without and the places where the current setup doesn’t suffice. You will, therefore, know where to apply the Odoo ERP software.
