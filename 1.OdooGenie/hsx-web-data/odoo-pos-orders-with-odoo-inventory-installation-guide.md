# Odoo POS Orders with Odoo Inventory | 6 Easy steps Installation Guide

The precise control of your inventory is most important to your successful retail business. For store owners, the POS is not just a method of accepting payments from customers, but a handy tool that provides you with valuable information regarding your sales and inventories. ## How to Install Odoo POS with Inventory

Odoo POS with Inventory installation is a simple job. Just read this manual, and you’ll be operating your systems in a matter of no time.


### Step 1: Install the Odoo POS Module
- Log in to your Odoo account with an Administrator username.
- Go to the Apps menu.
- Find the Point of Sale module and click on Install.
- After installing, the POS module will be located in the main dashboard.

### Step 2: Configure Your POS Setup
- Go to the POS module and select Configuration from the drop-down.
- Go to Point of Sale, and choose Create to start creating your POS system.
- Give your POS a name (e.g., “Main Store Register”).
Personalize options such as payment methods, printing receipts, and reward programs based on the need of your business.

Tip: Odoo allows you to sell online and offline. Offline sale data are logged on your system and synchronize as soon as you are back online.

### Step 3: Install and Configure the Inventory Module

- Return to the Apps menu and search for the Inventory module.
- Install the module and view it on the main dashboard.
- Organize your warehouse locations in the Inventory area by navigating to Configuration &gt; Warehouses.
- Ensure that your inventory management matches the configuration of your physical store(s).

### Step 4: Integrate Your POS with Inventory
- Return to the POS module and choose Settings.
- Activate Inventory Management. This ensures that sales in POS update your levels of stock immediately.
- Select the stock location or warehouse which you would like your POS to draw from (e.g., Main Warehouse).
- This integration will now enable the POS system to automatically deduct sold products from your inventory and create accurate stock reports. This will be a tremendous time-saver for hours checking.

### Step 5: Add Products to Your POS

- Proceed to the Inventory page and ensure that you have your products listed correctly.
- Each product entry must include significant details like the SKU, name, stock, price, and barcode (where applicable).
- Go back to the POS module and check that products are syncing properly. Products will now be on sale at the register.

### Step 6: Check Your Setup
- Test with a test transaction via the POS system.
Verify the Inventory module to ensure that the inventory quantities have been correctly updated.
- Prepare a sales report in order to ensure that all information is flowing effortlessly between both sides.
- And that’s all! Your Odoo POS and Inventory system is now integrated, giving you an end-to-end solution to manage your retail business more efficiently.
