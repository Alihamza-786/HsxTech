## What is Odoo Barcode Reader?

Odoo Barcode Reader is a component of the Odoo ERP. It is designed to simplify warehouse and retail work. It is fully compatible with Odoo’s robust inventory management and sales capabilities. This assists in tracking products effectively and fault-free.
If you are scanning new stock, selecting products for orders, or taking a complete inventory count, the Odoo Barcode Reader does away with manual data entry. Scanning rapidly, you can see real-time information instantly, which makes you more efficient and accurate.

## Simple Steps to Install Odoo Barcode Reader

### Step 1: Install the Odoo Barcode Module.
- Start by installing the Odoo Barcode app through your Odoo dashboard.
- Simply go to the Apps module and enter “Barcode.”
- After you’ve located it, click on install. This installation sets the stage for the use of barcode scanning features.


### Step 2: Install the Barcode Module
- Navigate to the Inventory page.
Go to Settings and enable the feature for Barcodes.
- Select the operations you want to perform with barcodes (e.g., tracking stock or shipping orders).
- The flexibility to adjust settings allows you to tailor the system to your individual work requirements.

### Step 3: Print Stock Barcodes
To assign barcodes to your products, follow the following steps:

- Navigate to the Inventory option and select Products.
- Select a product, and then click on the “Barcode” button.
- Get the barcode image and print it with a label printer.
- You can affix these barcodes on products, storage containers, or shipping boxes.


### Step 4: Select a Compatible Barcode Scanner
Odoo Barcode Reader will work with almost any scanner with USB or Bluetooth interface. Make sure your scanner is correctly connected to your device, so your Odoo system will be able to communicate at high speed.


### Step 5: Begin Scanning Jobs
Once your barcodes are printed and your scanner is ready, you can now start scanning. Below are some of the most common uses for it immediately:

- Inventory Counts: Open the Barcode app, select “Inventory Adjustment,” scan your products, and watch Odoo update the records automatically.
- Order Fulfillment: Utilize the Barcode app to rapidly pick and ship orders.
- Stock Transfers: Place transfer orders, read the barcodes of products going out, and track the movement easily.

### Step 6: Watch and Improve
Use Odoo’s live dashboards to monitor how your barcode operations are progressing. By viewing scanner logs and stock movement, you will be able to gather valuable information to improve your process.
