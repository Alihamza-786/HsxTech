# Stock Replenishment in Odoo 18 Inventory | How to manage stock refills in Odoo Inventory

Effective inventory management is critical in the operation of a successful business. It assists your business in maintaining the right quantity of what you require and what you possess. For Odoo users, the stock replenishment function is the most effective method of maintaining your inventory without stress. As an inventory manager seeking to improve your operations or a business owner maintaining your expenses, understanding how Odoo Inventory operates for stock replenishment will definitely keep you ahead of the pack.
This blog post discusses why good stock replenishment matters, presents Odoo Inventory, and demonstrates how to utilize its rules of replenishment. ## Why Maintaining Stock Full Is Crucial

Effective replenishment of stocks is crucial to an effective inventory system. Below are why that is the case:

### 1. Prevent Lost Sales
Nothing annoys customers more than discovering that the product they need is out of stock. Quick restocking reduces the chances of stockouts, allowing you to fulfill customer needs and prevent losing sales to other competitors.

### 2. Reduced Holding Costs
Having excess stock can increase the cost of holding it, such as storing it and insuring it. Adding more stock at the appropriate time helps you better control inventory costs.

### 3. Proper Forecasting
When businesses manage restocking well, they can spot trends and predict future inventory needs better. This helps them make better decisions throughout the organization.
Having established why it is needed, the next is how Odoo Inventory will assist in re-stocking.

## Shelving Replenishment Rules in Odoo

Odoo’s replenishment rules assist you in replenishing in a planned manner, thus you have the correct products at the correct time.

### Step 1: Prepare Products for Restocking
- Proceed to the Inventory Module.
- Select a product that you would like to order a replenishment for, or add a new product.
- In the configuration of the product, tell the supply chain information. This is whether the product is Buy, Manufacture, or Replenish on Order (MTO).


### Step 2: Define Replenishment Rules
Odoo replenishment rules allow you to set thresholds at which the stock must be replenished. This is how:

- Proceed to Reordering Rules in Inventory Settings.
Create a new rule for the product selected.
- Define parameters such as:
Minimum Quantity: How much you should have so you need to order more.
- Maximum Quantity: The target amount of stock after replenishment.
- Quantity to Order: How much to order when you are replenishing.Save the rule, and it is now usable!


### Step 3: Use the Odoo Scheduler
Odoo’s scheduler assists with automatically executing your restocking policies. Activated, it regularly checks for stocks and orders a purchase order or manufacturing order based on maintaining a stock level range that you determined.
Having established these guidelines enables you to focus less on daily levels of inventory and more on your main business objectives.


## Manual vs. Automated Replenishment

### Manual Replenishment
Good for business with simple inventory requirements, manual restocking provides maximum control over stock decisions. For instance, you may analyze seasonality trends or manually modify stock orders when demand suddenly fluctuates.
But restocking is labor-intensive and subject to human error, especially for firms that are dealing with a huge product catalog.

### Automated Replenishment

Automatic restocking is very useful for larger or more intricate companies. When rules are defined in Odoo Inventory, restocking is an easy process. Automatic orders are generated by the system to keep your stock within the ranges you’ve defined, reducing errors and work for your people considerably.
