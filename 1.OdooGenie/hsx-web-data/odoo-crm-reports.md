# Odoo CRM Reports: How to Configure Reports for Small Business Success

Odoo CRM comes with strong reporting features to enable you to better understand your sales performance and activities. Having these reports set up is a simple process if you do the following.

## Step 1: Getting to the Reporting Section

First, log in to your Odoo CRM dashboard. On the navigation bar, you will have a “Reports” or “Sales Dashboard” option, depending on the version you are using. Here, you can select pre-built templates or design custom reports according to your objectives.

## Step 2: Selecting Your Report Type

Sales Reports – Analyze total sales, customer acquisition trends, or performance by product/service.
Pipeline Reports – Track the developments of deals throughout every step in your sales funnel.
Activity Reports – See how effective sales activities such as calls, meetings, and emails are.
Odoo CRM also accommodates sophisticated breakdowns of data by time frames, sales teams, areas, and others.


## Step 3: Tailoring Reports to Your Requirements

Customizing CRM reports is one of Odoo’s most powerful features. Here’s how you can do it:
Choose Filters – Apply dynamic filtering to add the most pertinent data to your objectives (e.g., filter by region or salesperson).
Group by Criteria – Segment your data in terms of significant categories, like deal size, type, or probability of winning.
Add Comparisons – Monitor your performance by comparing numbers between months or quarters to find trends.

## Step 4: Visualizing Data

Odoo CRM allows you to display data in charts, graphs, or in-depth tables:
Pipeline Charts – Ideal for monitoring deals through stages in your sales pipeline.
Bar Graphs – Contrast sales performance of various teams or regions.
Pie Charts – View revenue distribution by product category or customer.

## Step 5: Exporting and Sharing Reports
After you’ve customized your report, you can export it in different formats (Excel, PDF, etc.) to share with stakeholders. Want to keep your team in the loop? Schedule reports to automatically send via email on a regular basis.

## Step 6: Reporting Integration Across Modules

If you’re using Odoo’s broader ERP system, connect your reports to other modules like Accounting or Inventory for a more comprehensive analysis.
