# SEO in Odoo Website: Optimize Your Site for Google; How to Make Your Odoo Website More Search Engine Friendly

You’ve set up your Odoo website. It looks sleek, the buttons work, and everything flows. But there’s just one problem – it’s not pulling in traffic. No visitors, no leads, no calls. Sounds familiar?

Here’s the deal: without solid SEO, your site is like a billboard in the desert. No one sees it.

The term “SEO in Odoo website” might sound technical, but it really just means this – setting up your site so Google knows what it’s about and can recommend it to the people who are searching. Whether you’re a startup hustler or managing a growing business, some basic SEO work can bring you more visibility, more credibility, and yes more business.

## What Makes Odoo a Bit Different?

Odoo isn’t your typical website builder. It’s actually part of a full suite of business tools – CRM, invoicing, sales, inventory, HR – you name it. The website module is just one piece of the puzzle, but it has what you need to start ranking.

You get:

- Customizable web pages
- A blog app
- Editable URLs
- Title and description fields
- Easy drag-and-drop page builder

But to really get results, you’ve got to take a few extra steps. Let’s go through them.

## Step 1: Clean Up the Tech Stuff (It’s Not as Boring as It Sounds)

### Fix Those URLs
No one wants to see /page?id=abc123. Use simple, keyword-rich links instead – like /organic-face-cream or /about-us. You can change these in the page settings.

### Make Sure It Is Mobile-Friendly
Your website must be mobile-friendly. Most people browse from their phones. Scroll through your site on your own phone and see if:

- Text is readable
- Buttons are easy to tap
- Nothing’s breaking or shifting weirdly

### Speed Things Up
People bounce if your site takes too long to load. You can:

- Shrink your images before uploading
- Avoid using too many animations
- Ask your developer about caching or using a CDN

### Add the Lock
That little padlock in the browser bar? That’s HTTPS. Make sure your site is secure. Google prefers it, and so do your visitors.

## Step 2: Tell Google What Your Pages Are About


This part is called on-page SEO. You don’t need to be a tech wizard for this – just keep things clear and relevant.

### Write Proper Titles and Meta Descriptions
Every page needs a title and a short blurb that shows up in search results.

Example:

- Title: Organic Skincare Products | Glow Naturals
- Meta: Explore our full range of organic skincare, handmade in small batches. Free shipping across UAE.

Include your main keywords naturally. No spammy stuffing.

### Use Headings
Think of H1s, H2s, and H3s like headlines and sub-headlines in a newspaper. Your main title should be an H1. Break up sections with H2s and H3s so it’s easy to scan.

### Describe Your Images
Google can’t “see” pictures. That’s where alt-text comes in. Use it to describe what’s in the image in a simple sentence.

### Link Things Together
Mention your products in your blog posts. Link from your About page to your Contact page. Use natural phrases like:

- marketing automation tools
- small business SEO checklist
- Odoo website tips for beginners

This keeps visitors moving through your site and helps Google understand how it all connects.

## Step 3: Write Stuff People Actually Want to Read

Odoo has a great blog feature – use it! Share what you know. Write articles that answer common questions or explain something useful in your industry.

Tips for blogging:

- Pick a topic that solves a real problem
- Make it easy to skim with headings and lists
- Add some visuals or screenshots
- Keep it between 800-1200 words

Good example topics:

- “How We Grew Our Business with Odoo”
- “Top Digital Marketing Tools for Small Business Owners”

Make it personal. Make it helpful.

## Step 4: Choose the Right Keywords (This Part Is Magic)

Think about what someone would type into Google if they were looking for what you offer.

Would they search for:

- “best HR software for small teams”?
- “affordable web design in Dubai”?

Use free tools like:

- Google Keyword Planner
- Ubersuggest
- AnswerThePublic

Use one main keyword per page or blog post. Sprinkle in a few related ones, but don’t overdo it.

## Step 5: Selling Something? Pay Attention to Product Pages

If you run an online store through your Odoo website, don’t ignore these:

- Use original product descriptions (never copy-paste)
- Add clear photos with alt text
- Include shipping, return, or FAQ info if relevant

If your products come in multiple colors or sizes, make sure the content isn’t getting duplicated across versions. You might need to use a canonical tag or tweak the wording.

## Step 6: If You're Local, Play the Local SEO Game

Running a local service or shop? Here’s how to show up for “near me” searches:

- Add your business to Google Business Profile
- Use keywords like “digital agency in Dubai” or “best Pakistani restaurant in Sharjah”
- Create a dedicated page for each location if you serve multiple areas

Ask happy customers for reviews. They help a lot.

## Step 7: Check Your Stats Often

Don’t just set things up and forget them. Track what’s working.

### Use Google Analytics
See:

- Where visitors are coming from
- Which pages keep them around
- What content they love (or bounce from)

### Use Google Search Console
It shows:

- What search terms people use to find you
- Pages with indexing issues
- Which content is climbing the ranks

## Final Word: SEO Isn’t Hard But It Is a Habit

The thing about SEO is that it’s not a one-time fix. It’s a mindset. It’s how you think about your website, your content, your business.

SEO in Odoo website doesn’t mean becoming an expert overnight. It just means you make smart, steady improvements over time. You write clearly. You stay consistent. You help your customers find what they’re looking for.

And eventually, Google helps them find you.
