# Odoo CRM Chrome Extension Privacy Policy | HSxTech

Privacy Policy for Odoo CRM Chrome Extension

Effective Date: 22nd January 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how HSxTech  (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Odoo CRM extension (“Product”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner.

## 1. Information We Collect
Our Product does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. We do not have access to any of the data you manage within your Odoo CRM account through our extension.

## 2. Usage of User Data
Since we do not collect or store user data, we do not use any personal information in connection with our services or products.

## 3. Sharing User Data
We do not share user data with any third parties. As there is no collection of personal or sensitive information by our Product, no information will be transmitted outside of your local environment unless specifically initiated by you through other applications.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Our Product may interact with third-party services within the scope of its functionality; however, we will never share any collected data from users’ interactions without explicit consent (which we do not require as we don’t collect such data).

## 5. Security Measures
We take appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access to or unauthorized alteration, disclosure, or destruction of information under our control; however again this pertains solely to metadata which does not involve actual user-related content.

## 6. Your Rights
As we don’t collect personally identifiable information (PII), there are no specific rights related to accessing or managing such PII under this Privacy Policy because such practices are beyond its scope concerning individual privacy rights governed by applicable laws like GDPR/CCPA etc.

## 7. Changes To This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time due to changes in regulations and best practices related to privacy protection principles; significant changes will be communicated through notifications clearly displayed on our website at www.hsxtech.net.

Contact Us If you have questions about this policy through the form on our contact page.
