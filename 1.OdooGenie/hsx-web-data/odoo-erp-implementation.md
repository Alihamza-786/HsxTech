Odoo ERP implementation refers to the integration of Odoo or its individual modules into an organization. The software is capable of meeting all business requirements and is widely regarded as the complete open-source ERP implementation software available today.

Odoo includes CRM, sales, project management, manufacturing, inventory management, accounting, human resource management, marketing activities, customer support tools, and other business applications.
