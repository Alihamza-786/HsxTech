## What is an ERP System?

An ERP system is integrated software with the main objective to streamline and automate core business functions. For the retailer, an ERP system plays an important role in managing every little thing, from their inventory to sales, finance, and human resources. It centralizes all your operations, provides real-time insights, and easily manages complex processes.


Odoo ERP will help a retailer improve customer service and lower costs by automating many manual processes. Some of the key functions of the system are as follows:

- Inventory management: This system provides an accurate level of stocks to avoid stockouts and overstocking. It also helps maintain efficient workflows with fast and accurate order fulfillment.
- CRM: Track customer information, monitor sales opportunities, and improve customer service.
- Financial management: Easy accounting, reporting, and compliance.
