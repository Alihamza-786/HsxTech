## Overview of Odoo 18's Inventory Reporting Features

To get into the specifics of how to reap the most value from the inventory reports provided by Odoo18. Take a look at the brief descriptions of the most popular available reports.

### 1. Stock Moves Report
It gives all the information regarding each inventory movement in the program. It permits you to keep track of when and how many items have been moved from one place to another and returned to sellers. Monitoring the movement of stock is vital to understanding how inventory is handled because it assists in identifying issues and determining if there are any issues that need to be addressed, such as inventory that is not being used or isn’t properly distributed.

### 2. Inventory Valuation Report
This report will reveal the worth of your inventory based on the price your company charges for its products and the price you pay for the items you sell. This report is very useful when making financial decisions to improve margins, decide whether it’s worth buying another item or set of items, or figure out what products are the most profitable.

### 3. Stock Level Report
If you’re trying to stop your inventory from selling out or you’re facing a shortage, this report will give you the state of each product that you offer to the public for sale. It will inform you of the condition of your inventory based on the anticipated demand, allowing you to make informed buying selections.

### 4. Product Performance Report

Are you interested in finding out which products are attracting the attention of others? Other items aren’t attracting attention. What’s the significance of this report? It’ll provide the current status of each product, along with margins of profit as well as variation in the sales. It’s an excellent instrument for determining the most sought-after items and those that aren’t performing similarly and altering your inventory plan to increase the amount of inventory you own.

### 5. Sales & Purchase Analysis Report

It offers more in-depth reports that allow you to analyze the details of sales and purchase information. When you look at the sales mix and purchases, you’ll be able to spot patterns, assess the effectiveness of the vendor you select to work with, and get more information about the condition of the inventory you’ve constructed. This kind of document, combined with Odoo’s ability to connect to other modules such as purchases and sales, will give all the required information needed to manage the business.
