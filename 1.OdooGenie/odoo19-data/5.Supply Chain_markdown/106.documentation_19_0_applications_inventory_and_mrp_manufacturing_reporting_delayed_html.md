# Delays

Odoo’s _Manufacturing_ app displays _delays_ in manufacturing orders through the Delayed Productions filter. If the MO’s end date exceeds its deadline, the deadline is highlighted in red to draw attention to the delay.

## Deadline calculation

The MO deadline depends on how the MO was created, and is calculated as follows:

  * **Make To Order** : the MO deadline is the _Sales Order Delivery Date_.

  * **Replenishment** : the MO deadline is _today + Manufacturing Lead Time_.

  * **Manually created MO** : the deadline field remains empty.

Important

The MO _deadline_ is not the same as the MO _end date_.

The end date is computed as:

\\[\text{End date} = \text{Scheduled start date} +\text{Total duration of all operations}\\]

## Filters

Several additional filters are available to help track delays:

  * Delayed Productions: the MO’s _scheduled start date_ is later than the deadline.

  * Late: the MO’s _scheduled end date_ exceeds the deadline, highlighted in red.

  * Late Availability: one or more required components are not available before the deadline. For example, a confirmed purchase order or manufacturing order for components is scheduled to end _after_ the MO deadline.

  * Components Available: all components are available to begin production.

## Use case

Consider an MO with a deadline of **September 17th** :

  * If production on the MO starts after September 17th, it appears in the Delayed Productions filter.

  * If required components are scheduled to arrive after September 17th, the MO appears in the Late Availability filter.

  * If the MO has a scheduled end date after September 17th, it appears in the Late filter.

By combining these indicators, planners can quickly identify where production is at risk of missing delivery commitments.
