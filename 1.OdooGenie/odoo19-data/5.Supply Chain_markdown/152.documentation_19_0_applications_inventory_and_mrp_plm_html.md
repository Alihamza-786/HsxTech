# Product lifecycle management

Odoo _Product Lifecycle Management_ (_PLM_) offers a systematic approach for testing, collaboration, and iteration of products across concept development, design, manufacturing, marketing, and post-launch support stages.

With approvals, key stakeholders can review changes before implementation.

  * Change management
    * Engineering change orders
    * ECO types and stages
    * Version control
  * Project management
    * Approvals
