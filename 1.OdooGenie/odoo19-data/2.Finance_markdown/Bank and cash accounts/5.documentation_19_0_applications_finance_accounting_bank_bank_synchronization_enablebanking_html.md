# Enable Banking

**Enable Banking** is a third-party provider aggregating banking information from bank accounts all in one place. It offers non-intrusive connectivity to ASPSPs’ official APIs across Europe without storing data.

**Odoo** synchronizes directly with banks to get access to all bank transactions and automatically import them into your database.

See also

  * Bank synchronization

  * Enable Banking website

## Configuration

### Link bank accounts with Odoo

  1. Start synchronization by clicking on Accounting ‣ Configuration ‣ Add a Bank Account;

  2. Select your bank;

  3. Make sure you give your consent to share your account information with Odoo by clicking Continue authentication;

  4. Finally, you are redirected to your bank’s login page.
