NETSOL is proud to announce our participation in the upcoming GlobalData Automotive Europe Conference and Awards 2025, taking place at the NH München Ost Conference Center in Munich, Germany, from October 15-16, 2025.
As a Silver Sponsor and Exhibitor, we are excited to join some of the most forward-thinking minds in the automotive and mobility sectors, sharing insights, showcasing innovation and celebrating the industry's brightest achievements.
This prestigious event brings together leading OEMs, auto captives, mobility service providers, fintech innovators and technology solution partners to explore the latest trends shaping the European automotive landscape. From electrification and connected vehicles to digital retailing and AI-driven finance, the conference serves as a hub for innovation and industry collaboration, and NETSOL Europe is honored to be part of it.
We’re thrilled to share that our digital automotive retail platform Transcend Retail has been shortlisted for ‘Best Technology Platform of the Year’ at the GlobalData Automotive Europe Awards.
Transcend Retail
is a next-generation platform designed to streamline the end-to-end vehicle purchase journey. It is the future of how OEMs, dealer groups and dealerships sell, finance and deliver cars. The proven solution turns complexity into clarity, friction into profit and the traditional purchase into an omni-channel experience that is simple and seamless for the customer and profitable for OEMs and dealers.
This nomination recognizes Transcend Retail’s transformational impact on automotive retail, helping our clients deliver intuitive, personalized and fully digital buying experiences that are essential in today’s competitive mobility landscape.

During Day One of the event (October 15), from 11:40 AM – 12:00 PM, our very own
Andy Page
, Senior Client Consultant at NETSOL Europe, will be delivering a session on ‘From Pressure to Performance: 5 Strategic Steps to AI Success’. He will share actionable strategies to help organizations build an AI-ready culture, identify high-impact use-cases, digitize and centralize data processes, navigate compliance risks and implement scalable AI solutions with confidence.
The whitepaper
AI roadmap for auto finance
provides the written companion to Andy's session framework, a five-step roadmap covering exactly the same priorities: building AI readiness, identifying high-impact use cases, and implementing scalable AI responsibly across auto finance operations.
On Day One, Andy will also be participating in a high-level panel discussion from 1 PM – 1:30 PM. The session, titled ‘Digital Transformation on Wheels: How Innovation is Reshaping Automotive Operations’, will bring together thought-leaders to explore how finance providers are adapting to the shifting demands of modern consumers and the technology that supports them.
Andy will draw on decades of experience and NETSOL’s global perspective to share key insights into:
Bridging legacy systems with agile, cloud-native platforms
Scaling digital finance operations across regions
Meeting customer expectations for frictionless, digital-first journeys
Preparing for the future of mobility ownership models
Other panelists part of the discussion include Thomas Nokin, Founder and CEO, Baskin, Nuno Silva, CEO, VTXRM - Software Factory and Christopher Schmitz, Partner Strategy and Transactions, EY-Partenon.
We invite attendees to join this engaging session to gain practical strategies and future-focused perspectives on digital automotive finance and mobility transformation.
