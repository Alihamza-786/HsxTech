NETSOL Technologies is proud to announce our participation as both Exhibitor and Lanyard Sponsor at the
2025 AFIA (Australian Finance Industry Association) Conference
, taking place on September 16th at Saltbox in Sydney.
Returning for its 29th year, the AFIA Conference is Australia’s premier financial services event, and this year’s theme, “Where Ideas Connect”, makes it the perfect stage to showcase how NETSOL is driving the future of finance with Transcend Finance and our newest innovation, CheckAI.
The AFIA Conference has long been the centerpiece of Australia’s financial calendar, a platform where policymakers, lenders, technology providers, and innovators come together to debate, share, and shape the industry’s future.
This year’s agenda features thought leaders, including:
Dr Sarah Hunter, Chief Economist and Assistant Governor (Economic), RBA
The Hon Dr Daniel Mulino MP, Assistant Treasurer & Minister for Financial Services
Paul Bloxham, Chief Economist (Australia, New Zealand and Global Commodities) & Managing Director, HSBC Australia
Dr Juliette Tobias-Webb, Behavioural Scientist & Professor of Practice at the AI and Cyber Futures Institute, and many more
These thought leaders will explore pressing topics such as AI, data, payments, fraud prevention, sustainability, leadership in the age of transformation, and much more. The conference will provide invaluable opportunities for attendees to gain insights, challenge perspectives, and discover new ways to drive the industry forward.
And with NETSOL as lanyard sponsor, every delegate will literally carry our brand throughout the day, a visible sign of our commitment to the
Australian market
. Find us at Booth 8.
At the heart of our showcase will be
Transcend Finance
, our industry-leading, AI-powered platform that is revolutionizing finance and leasing operations. With the ever-increasing complexity of financial services, Transcend Finance offers a unified, scalable, and flexible platform that empowers financial institutions, auto captives, and commercial lenders to streamline operations, reduce costs, and enhance the customer experience.
As financial institutions face heightened regulatory demands, rising customer expectations, and the need for faster, more efficient processing, Transcend Finance delivers by enabling:
Faster decision-making:
Automate manual processes, enabling quicker credit approvals and document processing.
Enhanced customer experience:
Deliver a seamless, omnichannel experience that enhances customer trust and satisfaction.
Operational efficiency:
Optimize workflows and scale operations without compromising on quality or service delivery.
Adaptive compliance:
Built to support regional regulatory frameworks, including Australian accounting and reporting requirements.
At AFIA 2025, we’ll showcase why leading global lenders, captives, and banks trust Transcend Finance to future-proof their operations, and why it’s the right choice for Australian institutions navigating today’s dynamic environment.
In addition to Transcend Finance, we are thrilled to introduce
CheckAI
, our AI-powered assistant that is redefining how credit analysts make decisions. CheckAI provides real-time, data-backed insights, automating key tasks that were previously manual, reducing human error, and improving the speed and accuracy of credit evaluations.
Key features of CheckAI include:
SWOT analysis:
Comprehensive insights into an applicant’s financial health, identifying strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
Automated document analysis:
Extracts critical data from financial documents to speed up decision-making.
Interactive chat interface:
Credit analysts can engage with CheckAI via a conversational interface, receiving tailored advice and insights.
In a market where accuracy, speed, and regulatory confidence define competitive advantage, CheckAI empowers Australian lenders to make faster, smarter, and more transparent credit decisions.
