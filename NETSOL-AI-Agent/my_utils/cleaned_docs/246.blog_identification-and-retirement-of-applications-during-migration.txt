Governance

Management of Multi-Cloud
Managing multi-cloud is challenging due to architectural complexity, disaster recovery planning, rising costs, and security policy management.

Step # 1: Beginning the Identifying Journey
Embarking on a migration journey can seem daunting, but it’s not frightening, trust us! We’ve got some tips to make the process a breeze. The first and foremost step is to take a closer look at your current IT landscape and identify the applications that have been the most dependable companions through thick and thin.
We all indeed have those “old friends” that have remained a solid rock throughout the challenging phase, and it’s essential to recognize their value.
To make the assessment process a little easier, we recommend creating a detailed inventory of your applications and categorizing them into three groups:
Treasures to Keep:
These applications are the most valued jewels of your cloud environment. They did and still do bring immense value to your operations, making the process more manageable every day. This means these priceless applications are the ones that deserve a prominent place in your new cloud environment.
Modest Companions:
These applications have potential but may need extra care and trimming to fit seamlessly into your new AWS migration strategy. They have the possibility to become invaluable assets with a cloud facelift, optimization, or right sizing.
Relics of the Past:
These applications have served their purpose, and it’s time to gracefully bid them farewell. Of course, they are not a burden, as they helped you in darker times. But now, the infrastructure can stand tall without them in your environment. Don’t let them hinder your progress any longer!
By taking a thoughtful approach to assessing your applications, you’ll be well on your way to a successful migration journey. With a positive attitude and a little effort, you’ll soon enjoy the benefits of a streamlined cloud environment.
