A legacy of innovation and global reach in automotive finance
For more than forty years, NETSOL has established itself as a trusted partner to leading global automotive brands, supporting finance and leasing operations across diverse markets. Our AI-powered automotive financial solutions are designed to meet the complex needs of enterprise clients, enabling them to manage large-scale portfolios, enhance efficiency, and streamline operations. This dedication to innovation has helped NETSOL earn a reputation for reliability and excellence in automotive finance worldwide.
NETSOL's leadership in the Chinese auto finance sector
NETSOL holds a significant presence in China, commanding a 75% share of the country’s auto finance sector. In an automotive market as dynamic as China’s, driven by trends like urbanization, shifting consumer preferences, and a growing interest in electric vehicles (EVs), NETSOL’s tailored solutions stand out. Our platforms comply with China’s specific regulatory and operational requirements, providing clients with a seamless experience that aligns with local laws and market demands.
