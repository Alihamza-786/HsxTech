1st place: Team Tricon (GIKI) – $3,000
Led by Asad Irfan, Team Tricon secured 1st prize by impressing the judges with their cutting-edge innovation - Minutes.AI, an intelligent real-time virtual assistant designed to transform meetings into productive, actionable sessions. By seamlessly integrating AI-driven problem-solving, the team set a new benchmark in automation and efficiency.

2nd place: Predictive Vision (LUMS) – $2,000
With Saif ur Rehman leading the charge, Predictive Vision showcased exceptional analytical thinking and technical expertise, securing a well-deserved second place in the competition with their smart agricultural application, F.A.R.M.
Project Details: F.A.R.M
F.A.R.M – The future of agriculture with a revolutionary model
F.A.R.M is a smart agricultural application designed to empower farmers with real-time, AI-driven insights for better crop management, weather forecasting, and market analysis. The app serves as a personalized farming assistant
integrating cutting-edge AI, NLP, and real-time data analytics to optimize agricultural productivity.
Key features & capabilities
1. Smart weather advisory:
Provides personalized weather advice based on the user’s location.
Guides farmers on when to water crops and when to hold off, optimizing irrigation schedules.
Predicts weather conditions using advanced AI-driven meteorological insights.
2. AI-powered plant disease detection:
Farmers can upload images of affected leaves, and the disease detection model will diagnose the issue.
Helps in identifying pest infestations and crop diseases early, allowing timely intervention.
3. Market insights & pricing intelligence:
Provides real-time market trends and price forecasts to help farmers sell their produce at optimal rates.
Advises on supply-demand dynamics, ensuring profitable crop selection for the next planting season.
4. Integration with agricultural research:
Leverages advanced agricultural research to provide farmers with insights on location-based soil conditions and climate adaptability.
Bridges the gap between farmers and technology, making modern agricultural practices more accessible.
Innovative technology stack
The app operates through an agentic approach, meaning it utilizes multiple intelligent AI agents that specialize in various agricultural tasks:
NLP-powered assistance:
Supports multilingual interaction, ensuring accessibility for farmers across different regions.
Plant disease detection agent:
Identifies and provides solutions for plant diseases using AI-powered image recognition.
Weather insights agent:
Offers real-time weather predictions and adaptive farming recommendations.
Market insights agent:
Not only provides market price trends but also advises farmers on which crops to harvest for maximum productivity and profitability.
Supply & demand analytics:
Uses historical and real-time datasets to predict optimal selling periods.
What sets F.A.R.M apart?
Unlike traditional agricultural advisory tools, F.A.R.M integrates real-time AI and geospatial technology to provide:
High-resolution mapping:
Helps in precision farming by analyzing soil conditions, crop health, and water availability.
Inventory & farm management:
Tracks crop cycles, fertilizers, and pest control measures.
Plant & pest management:
Detects pest infestations, nitrogen levels, and disease outbreaks using Generative AI.
LIVE API integration:
Connects with real-time agricultural databases to ensure farmers receive updated market and climate data.

Business & economic impact
For farmers:
Access to real-time market data increases profits by 20-30%.
Reduces post-harvest losses below 20%, ensuring better income stability.
For the economy:
Strengthens Pakistan’s agriculture sector, which contributes 19% of GDP and employs 38.5% of the workforce.
Enhances crop planning and food security
supporting national agricultural sustainability.
For investors & stakeholders:
scalable, high-impact model with potential for nationwide and global expansion
Drives growth in an underserved sector

3rd place: The Chateaus (NUST) – $1,000
Guided by Asfand Yar Jamali, The Chateaus secured a well-earned third place with their innovative AI-driven project, Prompt.AI, an AI-powered chatbot solution that automatically trains itself on relevant business data and generates ready-to-use chatbot scripts. This ensures businesses can seamlessly engage customers while transforming how information is accessed and utilized.
Project details: Prompt.AI
The system operates by integrating directly with a designated URL, which serves as a data source for the chatbot. This allows it to fetch and access relevant content directly from web pages, eliminating the need for manual updates.
Key features:
Customizable chatbot messages
– Tailor interactions based on business needs.
Brand personalization
– Integrate custom colors, logos, and themes.
Seamless data extraction
– Leverages a web scraper to extract and structure data efficiently.
AI-powered data processing pipeline
1. Web scraping & Data extraction:
Retrieves text, metadata, and essential content from the provided URL.
Filters out irrelevant data while preserving valuable information.
2. Text chunking & vectorization:
Breaks extracted content into meaningful chunks for efficient storage and retrieval.
Converts structured data into a numerical database, enabling GPT-4 to analyze user queries with high accuracy.
Impact & unique selling proposition
Traditional customer interactions can take up to 6 minutes per query. With Prompt.AI, this is reduced to just 1 minute, achieving a 5-minute time savings per interaction
For a website handling 100 queries per day, this translates to:
500 minutes saved daily
(~8.3 hours)
250 hours saved per month
30-50% workload reduction
Business value & cost efficiency
Prompt.AI enables businesses to cut operational costs significantly, offering:
Annual cost savings of up to $50,000 per website
Optimized resource allocation, allowing teams to focus on strategic initiatives instead of routine customer queries
Current & future integrations
Currently supports:
URLs, PDFs, and text files
Future roadmap:
Expanding to YouTube, Shopify, audio files, and more
Prompt.AI isn’t just an AI chatbot; it’s a scalable, intelligent, and cost-effective solution that reshapes the future of automated customer support.

AI is no longer constrained by local challenges—what we create today can have a global impact. The ability to move from idea to execution quickly is reshaping how we think about development. This hackathon reinforced that AI innovation is universal, and nothing stops Pakistan or any other ecosystem from competing on a global stage.
- Faizaan Ghauri, Chief Strategy Officer, NETSOL Technologies Inc.
