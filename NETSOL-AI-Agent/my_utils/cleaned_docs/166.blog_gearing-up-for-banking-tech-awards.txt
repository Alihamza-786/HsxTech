The
‘Best as-a-Service Solution’
category spotlights platforms that deliver measurable value through flexible, cloud-based service models. Transcend Retail stands out in this category by offering an agile, scalable and modular solution tailored for OEMs, dealer groups and dealerships.
Rather than offering a one-size-fits-all product, Transcend Retail embraces the
‘as-a-Service’
model in the truest sense — allowing clients to adopt only the components they need, integrate them effortlessly with existing infrastructure and scale operations without disruption. Built on an API-first, micro-services architecture, the platform delivers a truly composable solution that can adapt to the unique demands of various markets and business models.
From inventory management and deal structuring to trade-in valuations and
digital contracting
, Transcend Retail covers the entire vehicle sales and financing journey — all delivered via the cloud, with enterprise-grade security and performance.
A major differentiator for Transcend Retail is its modular and interoperable design. It integrates seamlessly with existing dealer management systems (DMS), customer relationship management (CRM) tools and third-party lender platforms. This ensures that users can deploy advanced capabilities without having to overhaul their core systems.
The platform also supports multi-brand and multi-lender environments, offering the flexibility to accommodate complex operational structures while maintaining consistency across channels. This makes Transcend Retail ideal for enterprise clients seeking scalable solutions without compromising agility or control.
OEMs
OEM-led digital retail transformation is at the core of what NETSOL delivers. Whether OEMs require a comprehensive end-to-end platform or seek to enhance an existing solution, Transcend Retail provides the technology necessary to execute and scale effectively. The platform empowers OEMs and their dealer networks with real-time insights, optimizes operations and ensures a seamless, connected retail experience. Importantly, it achieves all of this without disrupting dealer operations or existing workflows.
Dealer groups
Transcend Retail enables dealer groups to optimize their operations for greater efficiency and profitability. Its modular architecture integrates seamlessly with DMS, CRMs and third-party lender systems, breaking down silos and streamlining workflows. The platform ensures smooth operations while providing advanced analytics and dashboards that offer real-time insights into inventory and profitability. These capabilities drive growth without disrupting day-to-day operations.
Dealerships
Transcend Retail equips dealers with the tools to accelerate sales, close deals more efficiently and capitalize on every profit opportunity. By enabling improved lead conversion, streamlined workflows and increased F&I revenue, the platform supports stronger business outcomes. Customer-facing features such as transparent pricing, AI-driven trade-in valuations and flexible financing options contribute to a seamless, user-friendly buying experience – resulting in higher conversion rates.
At its core,
Transcend Retail is built to deliver results
. For OEMs, it facilitates unified digital retail strategies that connect national and regional sales efforts. For dealer groups, it enhances lead conversion, reduces friction in the sales process, and boosts Finance and Insurance (F&I) revenue. And for customers, it creates a modern, intuitive buying journey with tools like AI-powered trade-in evaluations, transparent pricing and flexible financing options, while ensuring a consistent experience across both online and offline channels.
These features not only contribute to better operational outcomes, but also enhance the Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) — an increasingly critical metric in the competitive landscape of automotive retail. By combining powerful back-end capabilities with customer-facing simplicity, Transcend Retail succeeds in transforming the automotive buying experience into a seamless, omni-channel interaction.
Transcend Retail is used by different customers in the United States, one of the most notable being MINI USA. MINI is an independent brand of the BMW Group. In the United States, MINI USA operates as a business unit of BMW of North America, LLC, located in Woodcliff Lake, New Jersey and includes the marketing and sales organizations for the MINI brand. The MINI USA sales organization is represented in the US through a network of 115 MINI passenger car dealers.
Patrick McKenna, the Department Head of Marketing, Product and Strategy at MINI USA, stated:
“In May 2021, MINI USA and MINI Financial Services set out to build a digital retail tool that could handle credit decisioning, eContracting and eSignatures to allow end-to-end transactions. For the past 3 years, major automotive forums in the industry have recognized MINI Anywhere (Powered by NETSOL’s digital retail platform) as one of the top ranked OEM digital retail tools in the competitive luxury space. We are extremely proud of MINI Anywhere, the team at NETSOL Technologies and want to thank our dealer partners for such an achievement.”
Mike Peyton, Chief Motorer and Vice-President of MINI of the Americas stated:
“Following the pandemic, MINI decided it was time to create an end-to-end purchasing and finance journey for the consumer. It was important to create something more than a lead generator to facilitate the complete transaction for any MINI customer, any place, any time. The usual suspects in the market could not offer that except for NETSOL.”
Since its debut, MINI USA's digital retail program, MINI Anywhere powered by NETSOL’s Transcend Retail technology, has established itself as a premier digital retail platform in the automotive industry. More than a sophisticated lead generator, MINI Anywhere offers MINI dealers an omni-channel, end-to-end sales platform while delivering customers a seamless yet comprehensive shopping experience.
From payment options aligned with MINI Financial Services programs to trade-ins, MINI finance and insurance (F&I) options, streamlined credit approvals, digital contracts and delivery schedules, every aspect has been designed with convenience in mind. Remarkably, MINI enthusiasts can finalize their purchase in as little as 30 minutes, all from the comfort of their homes.
In the 12 months from 2023-2024, the program has more than doubled dealer enrollments now active across nearly two-thirds of the MINI USA dealership network, enabling an unprecedented 5x increase of lead volume and vehicle sales, and an impressive 20% lead conversion rate on average. The program continues to roll out across the network.
