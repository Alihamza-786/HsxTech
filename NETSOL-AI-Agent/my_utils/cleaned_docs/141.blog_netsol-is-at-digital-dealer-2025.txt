The automotive retail industry is at a turning point in the United States, a dynamic evolution fueled by digital transformation, consumer expectations and data-driven innovation. As dealerships, OEMs and tech providers gather at the Digital Dealer Conference and Expo 2025 in Las Vegas this coming week, NETSOL is proud to take center stage as an exhibitor, showcasing its proven, cutting-edge digital retail platform Transcend Retail that is reshaping the future of automotive retail.
At NETSOL, we don’t just observe the industry’s evolution, we help drive it. With over four decades of experience delivering enterprise technology solutions to the global automotive finance and leasing industry, we expanded our reach into digital automotive retail, helping modern dealerships elevate the car-buying experience.
Digital Dealer brings together thought-leaders, disruptors and decision-makers from across the automotive ecosystem. This year, the event is sharper than ever – focused, streamlined and designed to give more time for genuine conversations, hands-on demos and tangible takeaways.
For us, it’s an opportunity to showcase our digital retail platform Transcend Retail and share real success stories from transformative collaborations with clients like MINI USA and new partnerships with clients like Sonic Automotive.
The key highlight at our booth this year will be Transcend Retail – our proven, modular digital retail platform that empowers OEMs, dealerships and dealer groups to deliver seamless, end-to-end online vehicle purchase experiences.
Transcend Retail
is not just a technology solution! It’s the future of how cars are sold, financed and delivered. It's a strategic enabler that turns complexity into clarity, friction into profit and a complex purchase into a simple and seamless process for the customer, all while maintaining profits for OEMs and dealers.
Built with flexibility at its core, it allows dealerships to:
Enable 100% digital, hybrid or showroom-assisted journeys
Offer AI-powered real-time credit decisioning and financing options
Integrate with inventory management and trade-in systems
Provide branded, frictionless user experiences on web or mobile
Maintain compliance with finance, insurance and privacy regulations
In today’s retail environment, where customers expect the same intuitive, personalized experiences they get from leading e-commerce platforms, Transcend Retail bridges the gap between dealership tradition and digital innovation.

Our work with MINI USA is a prime example of what’s possible when innovation meets execution. With Transcend Retail, MINI USA successfully launched a first-of-its-kind digital retailing experience, enabling customers to complete the entire vehicle purchasing process online – in as little as 30 minutes, all from home.
MINI USA's digital retail program
, MINI Anywhere, powered by NETSOL's Transcend Retail technology, has become a leading platform in the automotive industry. More than just a lead generator, it offers MINI dealers an omni-channel, end-to-end sales solution and provides customers with a seamless shopping experience.
With features like payment options aligned with MINI Financial Services, trade-ins, F&I choices, streamlined credit approvals, digital contracts and delivery scheduling, the platform is designed for ultimate convenience.
In the 12 months from 2023-2024, the program more than doubled dealer enrollments now active across nearly two-thirds of the MINI USA dealership network, enabling an unprecedented 5x increase of lead volume and vehicle sales, and an impressive 20% lead conversion rate on average. The program continues to roll out across the network. We’re not just talking about potential – we’re showcasing real-world success.
In a bold move underscoring NETSOL’s growing influence in digital automotive retail, Sonic Automotive, a Fortune 500 company and one of the largest automotive and powersports retailers in the United States, selected NETSOL to help drive the development of a next-generation digital retail platform for its EchoPark Automotive division.
This
strategic collaboration
has commenced as a discovery engagement, led by NETSOL’s Transcend Retail team in close partnership with key Sonic stakeholders. The initiative aims to design a future-state blueprint that reflects Sonic’s ambitious vision: to deliver a modern, technology-enabled retail experience that elevates both customer satisfaction and operational efficiency across the EchoPark network.
This forward-looking partnership is more than a tech project; it represents a shared commitment to redefining the car-buying journey through AI, automation and seamless system integration. As the automotive retail sector accelerates its digital evolution, this engagement stands as a significant milestone, not only for NETSOL and Sonic Automotive, but for the broader industry’s shift towards scalable, customer-first innovation.
