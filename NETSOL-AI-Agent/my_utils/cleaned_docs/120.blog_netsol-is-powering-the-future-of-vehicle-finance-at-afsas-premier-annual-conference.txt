One platform. The entire finance lifecycle.
At Booth 4, attendees will get a firsthand look at
Transcend Finance
, NETSOL’s proven, next-generation platform purpose-built to manage the entire lifecycle of retail and wholesale vehicle financing –all on one unified system.
Transcend Finance empowers lenders to move beyond traditional financing and orchestrate the broader mobility ecosystem through an AI-enabled, API-first platform. With a proven track record of delivering complex implementations and deep insight into OEMs, it provides a trusted, low-risk foundation for transformation. Transcend unifies wholesale, retail and emerging mobility services, accelerates decisioning and automates complex workflows, driving faster onboarding, higher satisfaction and scalable growth.
From start to finish, Transcend Finance delivers seamless control and intelligence across:
Application intake and onboarding
Credit evaluation and approvals
Contract origination and management
Disbursement and funding
Servicing, billing, and customer management
Collections and end-of-term processes
Designed for scale, configurability and regulatory readiness, Transcend Finance empowers lenders to operate faster, smarter and more efficiently, without sacrificing control or compliance. Orchestrate every stage of dealer funding and customer lending, eliminating fragmentation across systems and departments. Gain real-time visibility into portfolio performance, utilization and delinquency trends for proactive decision-making.
Trusted by world-leading financial institutions, auto captives, commercial financiers and diverse finance and leasing enterprises, Transcend Finance is actively transforming vehicle finance operations across North America and global markets.
The
MINI Anywhere case study
demonstrates the scale of that impact in the US market, MINI USA achieved a 23% increase in average conversion rate from lead to sales over three years, establishing a benchmark for what NETSOL-powered digital retail and finance delivers in a production OEM environment.
Intelligent credit decisioning. Reimagined
Also on display at Booth 4 is
Check
: NETSOL’s AI-enabled credit decisioning engine that’s redefining how credit and funding teams work.
Check streamlines and enhances the entire credit evaluation lifecycle by:
Automating manual and repetitive underwriting tasks
Aggregating data from multiple internal and external sources
Processing complex financial documents with speed and accuracy
Performing in-depth financial and risk analysis
Accelerating decision timelines while improving underwriting precision
By combining advanced analytics with intelligent automation, Check empowers financial institutions to work smarter, faster and with greater confidence, turning complexity into clarity and insight into action.
While Check streamlines and speeds up underwriting, it is built around a human-in-the-loop framework that preserves expert oversight at every step of the decision-making journey. This approach helps mitigate bias, strengthens regulatory compliance and upholds exceptional standards of accuracy and fairness. Through an intuitive chat-based interface, analysts can engage directly with the AI, asking questions, exploring insights and fine-tuning decisions in real-time.
In an environment where speed and accuracy are competitive differentiators, Check is a game-changer.
