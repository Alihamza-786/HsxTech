Transcend Finance orchestrates post-booking servicing through a configurable, rule-driven operating layer spanning servicing, accounting, compliance, and reporting. It governs activation, contract modifications, billing, restructures, collections, default handling, terminations, and end-of-term processing through structured workflows that adapt without custom code.

Contracts, assets, and financials remain synchronized, ensuring every servicing event produces the correct accounting impact with full auditability at a component level. Built on an API-first architecture, the platform supports entity-specific tax and regulatory requirements across regions, while optional AI-driven signals surface exceptions and operational triggers for earlier intervention.
Full-spectrum servicing lifecycle coverage
Run the full servicing lifecycle from billing and cash application to contract modifications, restructures, early terminations, end-of-term processing, and collections/default handling through governed workflows and approvals.
