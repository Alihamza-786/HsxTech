• Charting the Course of Action
A guiding star is vital to making informed decisions, such as consolidated data. Before retiring any applications, gathering comprehensive data about your current system is essential. Analyzing performance metrics, usage patterns, and application dependencies is also important. This data-driven approach will give you a clear picture of which applications are crucial and which can be bid farewell.
By taking a thoughtful approach to assessing your applications, you’ll be well on your way to a successful migration journey. With a positive attitude and a little effort, you’ll soon enjoy the benefits of a streamlined cloud environment.
• Setting Priorities
When scrutinizing the data, it’s essential to classify applications according to their importance to your business. You can do this by placing them into three categories: “Mission-Critical,” “Supportive,” and “Redundant.”
Mission-critical applications are those that your business heavily depends on.
Supportive applications contribute to value but may have substitutes.
Redundant applications are those that are no longer necessary due to duplicated functionality.
Prioritizing these applications will assist you in focusing on what is most important during the migration process, and retiring them will become easier.
• In-depth analysis and AWS Discovery Tools
Discovery tools help you comprehensively inventory your applications, infrastructure, and interdependencies. These tools reveal the hidden glories in your old system, allowing you to identify redundant or underutilized applications that can be retired.
• Evaluate Crucial Interactions
Assessing how different applications interact and depend on each other is crucial. Retiring an application that plays a vital role in the ecosystem could cause disruptions throughout the system. Using data-driven discovery tools will help identify these dependencies and plan a migration that won’t cause disruptions.
• Consider User Experience
Consider how it might affect your business processes and user experience when considering application retirement. Talk to stakeholders to learn about their needs and expectations. If retiring an application will hurt productivity or customer satisfaction, it’s worth exploring other solutions.
• Retire, Archive, or Rehost: Choose Wisely
Some applications may not be necessary and can be retired completely, while others may be kept for later reference. If there are applications that are still useful but don’t fit your current on-premises setup, transferring them to AWS might be a good choice.
• Don’t Underestimate the Power of Communication
Finally, it is crucial to maintain open and transparent communication with all stakeholders throughout the assessment process, retirement decisions, and the benefits of cloud migration. By keeping them informed, you can ensure a united team that will guide your migration efforts toward success.
